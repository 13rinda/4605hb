Civil Rights Advocate

Brinda Mailvaganam approaches challenges with a solutions mindset. As a Civil Rights Advocate, she provides free guidance to help community members navigate civil and administrative matters, with a focus on immigration, labour rights, and fraud.

She helps affected individuals understand processes, prepare documents, and connect with the right resources to address their issues effectively. Her work is designed to empower the community and improve access to accurate, responsible support.

Brinda consults reputable legal guidance, including Charness Law, to ensure her knowledge is accurate and responsible. Brinda draws on her first-hand experience navigating civil processes as well as numerous consultations with lawyers on a variety of matters. She is a skilled researcher and is adept at finding accurate, relevant, and practical resources to support community members, ensuring they have the information and guidance needed to navigate complex administrative and civil issues.
